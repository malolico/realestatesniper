## 3. Strategic Program Mission (Discovery result)

**Official Mission:**

> **Publication** is the evolution in which **authorized downstream outcomes** become eligible for **controlled outward disclosure** under governed eligibility and unit discipline — preserving Factory as producer of truth, Decision as upstream Decision sovereignty, and Product/Marketplace as distinct frontiers — without exposing raw Factory ELR, without rewriting upstream truth, and without absorbing Continuous Operation or Scale Out as SP06 facts.

This instantiates the Evolution Mandate SP06 purpose and capability type:

- **Purpose (Mandate):** Governed publication of outcomes downstream of Decision/Product — authorized, eligible, and separated from Factory ELR and from Marketplace calling Factory for tiering.
- **Capability type (Mandate):** Governed publication / eligibility / outward release posture downstream of Decision and Product frontiers.

**Core strategic boundary (binding):**

```text
Factory         = READ-ONLY producer truth (ELR / CB-16)
Decision (SP05) = CLOSED upstream Decision sovereignty / dossier
Publication    = governed outward disclosure (this program)
```

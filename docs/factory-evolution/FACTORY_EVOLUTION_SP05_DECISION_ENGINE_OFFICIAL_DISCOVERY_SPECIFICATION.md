## 3. Strategic Program Mission (Discovery result)

**Official Mission:**

> **Decision Engine** is the evolution in which a **sovereign Decision consumer**, **outside Factory internals**, consumes Factory **Decision Packages** at the constitutional handoff frontier **ST-RDY → ST-DEC**, exercising **decision authority** without repairing, rewriting, or upgrading Factory truth — and without absorbing Product, Marketplace, Publication, Continuous Operation, or Scale Out as SP05 facts.

This instantiates the Evolution Mandate SP05 purpose and capability type:

- **Purpose (Mandate):** Establish the Decision Engine as the sovereign decision consumer of Factory Decision Packages at ST-RDY → ST-DEC, preserving Factory as producer and Decision Engine as decision authority outside Factory internals.
- **Capability type (Mandate):** Decision-side consumption of Factory handoff packages; decision sovereignty at the Factory↔Decision frontier.

**Core strategic boundary (binding):**

```text
Factory         = sovereign producer of truthful Decision Packages
Decision Engine = sovereign consumer outside Factory internals
```

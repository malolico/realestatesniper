**Official Mission (Discovery result):**

> **Intelligence Alive** is the evolution in which Factory intelligence **analyses real, legitimate and traceable knowledge** produced or admitted by the existing Factory — under supervised-AI and fail-closed rules already constitutionalized — consuming published motors, loops, orchestration intelligence, and AI-assist layers **without** redesigning them and **without** claiming Decision Engine, Product, Marketplace, Publication, Continuous Operation, or Scale Out.

- **Purpose (Mandate):** Activate Factory intelligence as a living supervised capability: motors, loops, orchestration intelligence, and AI-assist layers operating under supervised-AI and fail-closed rules already constitutionalized.

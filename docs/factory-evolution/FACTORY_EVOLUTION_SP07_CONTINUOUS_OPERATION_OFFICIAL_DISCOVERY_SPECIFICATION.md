## 3. Strategic Program Mission (Discovery result)

**Official Mission:**

> **Continuous Operation** is the evolution in which Factory progresses from a system that can be **constructed, validated, and closed program-by-program** into an **institution capable of remaining continuously operable** under its constitutional, evidence, governance, auditability, and safety rules — preserving predecessor truth, fail-closed behavior, and operational honesty without treating every day as a construction reboot, and **without** absorbing Scale Out, Product, Marketplace, Publication delivery, or commercial entitlement as SP07 facts.

This instantiates the Evolution Mandate SP07 purpose and capability type:

- **Purpose (Mandate):** Sustain Factory as a continuously operable institution: watch/update/archive postures, operational continuity, governance, auditability, and ongoing operation without treating every day as a construction reboot.
- **Capability type (Mandate):** Ongoing operational continuity, governance persistence, watch/update/archive-style continuous Factory operation under constitutional rules.

**Core strategic boundary (binding):**

```text
Factory (SP01–SP03)     = living knowledge / operability foundation
Intelligence (SP04)     = CLOSED supervised intelligence posture
Decision (SP05)         = CLOSED Decision sovereignty · READ-ONLY
Publication (SP06)      = CLOSED eligibility/unit posture · delivery NOT_AUTHORIZED
Continuous Operation    = institutional persistence / continuity (this program)
Product / Marketplace   = OUTSIDE bounded SP07 core
Scale Out (SP08)        = LATER PROGRAM
```

```text
SP06 established bounded Publication eligibility/unit discipline.

```

This document creates the strategic layer named **FACTORY EVOLUTION PROGRAMS**.

- Construction Blueprint  
- CB-00…CB-19  
- Factory Core Hardening (**PROGRAM 01**)

Architecture already constructed and integrated remains authoritative on its own plane. Evolution Programs organize **future strategic direction** above that plane.

---

## 1. Purpose of Factory Evolution Programs

**FACTORY EVOLUTION PROGRAMS** is the official strategic evolution roadmap of RealEstateSniper Factory 2.0 after construction closure and after the Hardening / Integration documentary record already established.

Its purpose is to give Director, Developer, and any AI assistant a single strategic map of **what Factory evolves toward next**, without confusing that map with construction sequence, hardening inventories, or P-INT engineering blocks.

Evolution Programs answer: *in what strategic order does Factory become operationally alive, territorially capable, knowledge-rich, intelligence-bearing, decision-ready, publication-capable, continuously operable, and scalable — without redesigning the architecture already validated.*

---

## 2. Relation to Construction

Construction (Blueprint · CB-00…CB-19) is **closed as construction**. Evolution Programs **consume** construction outputs; they do **not** reopen CB phases as construction IMPL and do **not** alter CB constitutional semantics.

Future work under Evolution Programs may enrich or adapt **through governed Mandates**, always respecting CB frontiers — never by rewriting the Construction Blueprint or CB ledger as if construction were incomplete.

---

## 3. Relation to Hardening

Factory Core Hardening (**PROGRAM 01**) and Integration Surface Hardening (**PROGRAM 02**) remain **distinct Hardening programs** on the quality / surface plane.

Evolution Programs **do not modify** Hardening Statuses, HQ catalogs, or Hardening dispositions. Strategic Program numbering (**Strategic Program 01…08**) is **not** identity-equivalent to Hardening **PROGRAM 01 / PROGRAM 02**.

Hardening protects and reconciles Factory quality. Evolution Programs set strategic evolution destinations. Both may coexist; neither absorbs the other by this Mandate alone.

---

## 4. Relation to Integration

Integration (I.x · II.x · Service Edge · Admin wiring · closed P-INT slices as recorded in Continuity) remains the integration record of adapters and frontiers.

Evolution Programs **do not rewrite** Integration Statuses or reopen closed integration blocks as pending IMPL. They may **require future Mandates** that sit on Integration surfaces already established — without treating Integration Master Plan Fases as deleted or replaced.

---

## 5. Relation to P-INT

P-INT blocks remain the **engineering integration catalog** under the Master Plan.

Evolution Programs **never modify** P-INT definitions, Statuses, Closeouts, or Mandates by existence of this document. Any future engineering that implements a Strategic Program must still pass Continuity protocol (Discovery → Plan → Audit → Mandate → IMPL) and, where applicable, cite the relevant P-INT or new Mandate — without renaming or collapsing P-INT into Strategic Programs.

---

## 6. Relation to Product

Product (catalog, pricing, `access_tier`, commercial classification sovereignty) remains **outside Factory**.

Evolution Programs do **not** move Product sovereignty into Factory. Strategic Programs that approach Decision or Publication frontiers must preserve the constitutional rule: Factory does **not** alone assign commercial classification / `access_tier`.

---

## 7. Relation to Marketplace

Marketplace remains **downstream of Product**, never a direct consumer of Factory internals for price/tier decisions.

Evolution Programs do **not** authorize Marketplace→Factory decision coupling. Publication and Scale programs, if ever Mandated, must respect Marketplace separation already established in Continuity and Master Plan.

---

## 8. Relation to Supabase

Supabase remains under Continuity **Stop Rule**. Evolution Programs do **not** authorize Supabase work by listing.

Any Strategic Program that would touch Supabase requires **separate explicit Director authorization** and Stop Rule compliance. ELR is not replaced by product `deals` tables.

## 9. Constitutional principles

Under this Mandate:

1. **Strategic Programs are the official strategic evolution roadmap** of Factory.  
2. **They never replace existing architecture** (Blueprint, CB, Hardening, Integration, P-INT, CCD, Continuity, Master Plan).  
3. **They never modify CB.**  
4. **They never modify Hardening.**  
5. **They never modify P-INT.**  
6. **All future development shall belong to one of the eight Strategic Programs.**  
7. **No Strategic Program may start until the previous one is closed**, unless the Director expressly authorizes an exception or a **demonstrable technical dependency** requires limited overlap — still under Mandate and Continuity protocol.  
8. Quality before volume; Evidence and Legitimacy mandatory; fail-closed; supervised AI; ELR ≠ deals; UI ≠ security; Product/Marketplace separated from Factory.  
9. Listing a Strategic Program **does not authorize IMPL**, push, Web, Supabase, or Continuity next-block selection by itself.  
10. Evolution proceeds by **adapters / enrichment / governed Mandates**, not parallel architectures (CCD Adición 7 spirit).

# The Eight Strategic Programs

## Strategic Program 01 — Factory Alive

**Official name:** Strategic Program 01 — Factory Alive  

**Purpose:**  
Establish Factory as an operable Factory system in its own right: Registry, ELR, orchestration, governance reads, and controlled operational surfaces function as a living Factory control plane without requiring Product or Marketplace to exist inside Factory.  

Factory Alive means Factory can be run, observed, and governed as Factory — not that Arizona production launch, national scale, or commercial publication are complete.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Operability and liveness of Factory core and already authorized integration control-plane surfaces; Factory-side readiness for supervised operational use under existing constitutional frontiers.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Product pricing / `access_tier`; Marketplace; Decision Engine commercial sovereignty; Supabase Stop Rule breach; CB redesign; Hardening rewrite; P-INT rewrite; Arizona jurisdictional live-world completion; national Scale Out.

---

## Strategic Program 02 — Arizona Alive

**Official name:** Strategic Program 02 — Arizona Alive  

**Purpose:**  
Bring Factory’s operational life into the Arizona territorial and jurisdictional reality already prioritized in Continuity and Integration records: local packs, jurisdiction-aware operation, and Arizona-facing Factory readiness as a living theater — without converting Factory into Product or Marketplace.  

Arizona Alive is territorial/operational activation of Factory for Arizona, not a rewrite of construction or a substitute for US legal / cybersecurity pre-launch reviews.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Jurisdiction-scoped operational capability for Arizona; alignment of Factory operation with Arizona as the active theater.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
National Scale Out; Product/Marketplace launch; CB redesign; Hardening/P-INT modification; unauthorized Supabase; replacement of mandatory pre-launch cybersecurity and US legal reviews.

---

## Strategic Program 03 — Knowledge Alive

**Official name:** Strategic Program 03 — Knowledge Alive  

**Purpose:**  
Make Factory’s knowledge posture live: Diamond / MPI / DDI-oriented knowledge completeness, evidence and legitimacy as living requirements, and Factory’s capacity to hold and advance property intelligence under constitutional Evidence/Legitimacy rules.  

Knowledge Alive concerns the vitality of Factory knowledge — not commercial classification by Factory alone and not Marketplace publication.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Knowledge completeness, evidence/legitimacy vitality, and governed knowledge advancement inside Factory’s constitutional knowledge mission.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Factory assigning `access_tier` / commercial bands as Product; Marketplace UX; Decision Engine Product sovereignty; CB redesign; Hardening/P-INT modification; Supabase as ELR substitute.

---

## Strategic Program 04 — Intelligence Alive

**Official name:** Strategic Program 04 — Intelligence Alive  

**Purpose:**  
Activate Factory intelligence as a living supervised capability: motors, loops, orchestration intelligence, and AI-assist layers operating under supervised-AI and fail-closed rules already constitutionalized.  

Intelligence Alive deepens Factory’s capacity to discover and reason under governance — it does not authorize autonomous business decisions or Product pricing.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Supervised intelligence execution and enrichment within Factory (motors/loops/orchestration/AI-assist under constitutional constraints).

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Autonomous commercial decisions; Decision Engine Product sovereignty; Marketplace; CB redesign; Hardening/P-INT modification; unsupervised agent autonomy as business authority.

---

## Strategic Program 05 — Decision Engine

**Official name:** Strategic Program 05 — Decision Engine  

**Purpose:**  
Establish the Decision Engine as the sovereign decision consumer of Factory Decision Packages at the constitutional handoff frontier (ST-RDY → ST-DEC), preserving Factory as producer of packages and Decision Engine as decision authority outside Factory internals.  

This Strategic Program names the decision sovereignty destination; it does not move pricing/`access_tier` into Factory motors.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Decision-side consumption of Factory handoff packages; decision sovereignty at the Factory↔Decision frontier.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Embedding Decision Engine inside CB motors as Product; Marketplace publication logic inside Factory; Factory-alone commercial classification; CB/Hardening/P-INT modification by this Mandate alone.

---

## Strategic Program 06 — Publication

**Official name:** Strategic Program 06 — Publication  

**Purpose:**  
Governed publication of outcomes downstream of Decision/Product — so what leaves the system is authorized, eligible, and separated from Factory ELR and from Marketplace calling Factory for tiering.  

Publication is the strategic destination for controlled outward disclosure of decided/productized outcomes, not raw Factory expediente exposure.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Governed publication / eligibility / outward release posture downstream of Decision and Product frontiers.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Factory writing Marketplace deals tables as ELR; investor public exposure of full ELR; CB redesign; Hardening/P-INT modification; bypass of II.1–II.6 trust/eligibility principles already established.

---

## Strategic Program 07 — Continuous Operation

**Official name:** Strategic Program 07 — Continuous Operation  

**Purpose:**  
Sustain Factory as a continuously operable institution: watch/update/archive postures, operational continuity, governance, auditability, and ongoing operation without treating every day as a construction reboot.  

Continuous Operation is institutional persistence of a living Factory, not Scale Out and not construction reopening.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Ongoing operational continuity, governance persistence, watch/update/archive-style continuous Factory operation under constitutional rules.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
National multi-jurisdiction Scale Out; Product/Marketplace fusion into Factory; CB redesign; Hardening/P-INT modification; silent Stop Rule breaches (Web/Supabase).

---

## Strategic Program 08 — Scale Out

**Official name:** Strategic Program 08 — Scale Out  

**Purpose:**  
Expand Factory’s living operation beyond the initial theater toward national (and further) scale **without degrading architecture**, per CCD national-expansion and anti-parallel-architecture principles.  

Scale Out is strategic expansion of an already living, continuously operable Factory — not a pretext to invent a second Factory architecture.

**Capabilities it incorporates (type):**  
Jurisdictional/national expansion capacity preserving constitutional architecture and quality purpose.

**What it does NOT incorporate:**  
Parallel Factory architectures; CB redesign as “scale”; Hardening/P-INT modification by naming; Product/Marketplace sovereignty inside Factory; skipping prior Strategic Programs without Director exception or demonstrable technical dependency.

## 1. Purpose

Constitute Strategic Program 02 — **Arizona Alive** as the official Discovery instrument that defines **what** territorial and jurisdictional activation of the already-alive Factory means for Arizona — and what it expressly does **not** mean — under Continuity §27 step 1 (Discovery).

SP02-01 exists so that any later Implementation Plan, Mandate, or IMPL for Arizona Alive can be measured against a published constitutional result, without this document selecting methods, inventing connectors, or authorizing engineering.

---

## 2. Strategic objective

Bring Factory’s operational life — already established under Strategic Program 01 — into the **Arizona territorial and jurisdictional reality** already prioritized in Continuity and Integration records: **local packs**, **jurisdiction-aware operation**, and **Arizona-facing Factory readiness as a living theater**, without converting Factory into Product or Marketplace.

Arizona Alive is **territorial / operational activation** of Factory for Arizona. It is **not** a rewrite of construction, **not** a second Factory architecture, and **not** a substitute for mandatory United States legal and professional cybersecurity reviews required before Arizona **production launch** (Continuity §25; Evolution Mandate SP02 exclusions).

---

## 3. Official definition of Arizona Alive

**Arizona Alive** means, exclusively under SP02:

> Factory 2.0, already operable as Factory under SP01 COMPLETE, is **jurisdictionally alive for Arizona** when an authorized Factory operator can, under existing constitutional frontiers and without Product/Marketplace fusion, **(A)** treat Arizona as the **active operational theater** for Factory operation, **(B)** exercise jurisdiction-scoped operational capability using **local packs** and **jurisdiction-aware** Factory posture already contemplated by Continuity / Integration / CCD, **(C)** reuse the single United States Factory platform (Runtime, Evidence, Legitimacy, Intelligence, Orchestration, constitutional motors) **without** duplicating those cores as an Arizona fork, and **(D)** do so **without** claiming Arizona production launch, national Scale Out, Product/Marketplace launch, CB redesign, Hardening/P-INT modification, unauthorized Supabase, or replacement of pre-launch cybersecurity and US legal reviews.

This definition **instantiates** the Evolution Mandate Strategic Program 02 purpose and capability type, and **aligns** with CCD §4.2 / Adición 4 (Arizona = first operational jurisdiction; adapters / sources / configurations; anti-duplication).

**Arizona Alive does not mean:**

- Arizona production launch complete  
- National Scale Out (Strategic Program 08)  
- Product or Marketplace launch  
- Live DSO / real HTTP connectors automatically authorized or closed (P-INT-02 Live remains a separate Integration row unless later expressly Mandated under Continuity)  
- Knowledge Alive / Intelligence Alive / Decision Engine / Publication / Continuous Operation complete (SP03…SP07)  
- Identity with Continuity/Hardening “PROGRAM 02”  
- Reopening of CB construction or SP01 Alive criteria as new SP02 invention

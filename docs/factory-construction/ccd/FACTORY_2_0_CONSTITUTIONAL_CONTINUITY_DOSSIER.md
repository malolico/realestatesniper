## 1. Identity and Purpose

This document is the **official constitutional continuity dossier** of RealEstateSniper Factory 2.0.

Its purpose is to establish the permanent operating law of continuity for Factory 2.0 so that Carlos, Manolo, and any future AI assistant can continue work without loss of authority, protocol, scope, or documentary coherence.

This CCD governs:

- how continuity is preserved;
- how documentary authority is interpreted;
- how blocks are opened, audited, closed, and not reopened;
- how GitHub is used as shared official continuity reference;
- how future evolution is governed without inventing architecture;
- how AI assistants must read, stop, ask, and avoid unconstitutional interpretation.

The permanent purpose of Factory continuity under this CCD is **not** to add more components, but to preserve and continuously improve:

- the quality of intelligence;
- the quality of evidence;
- the quality of legitimacy;
- the capacity to discover real opportunities;
- the quality of Deal / Premium / Diamond classification as a system objective;
- the capacity for national expansion without degrading the architecture.

This purpose **does not** authorize Factory alone to assign commercial classification or `access_tier`. Product, Decision, and Marketplace sovereignties remain outside Factory as already established by the official corpus.

This CCD is:

- **constitutional-operational**, not a Discovery;
- **definitive continuity law**, not an Implementation Plan;
- **not** a Mandate;
- **not** a Status;
- **not** a Closeout;
- **not** a replacement for executable evidence.

This CCD does **not**:

- replace Factory domain constitution;
- replace construction sequence authority;
- replace integration roadmap authority;
- replace Status or Closeout evidence;
- authorize Factory engineering implementation by its existence alone.

## 2. Source Hierarchy

Continuity authority is governed by the following hierarchy.

### 2.1 Binding hierarchy

1. **Executable technical truth**  
   Code, validators, and verified Git state determine the real technical state.

2. **Explicit Director decisions and issued Mandates**  
   These govern authorization, scope, stop rules, parking, and operational direction.

3. **Domain constitutional rules**  
   Auditoría Maestra / FFO / OMC / related constitutional corpus remain non-annullable by later narrative.

4. **Status and Closeout documents**  
   These determine what a block officially delivered and how it officially closed.

5. **Blueprint and Integration Master Plan**  
   These determine construction sequence, integration gates, and design boundaries.

6. **This CCD**  
   This document governs continuity law: how to continue, authorize, close, read, and preserve coherence.

7. **State continuity narrative and secondary chat context**  
   These may preserve handoff context, but do not supersede higher authorities.

### 2.2 Class 1 / 2 / 3 material

- **Class 1**: explicit Director decisions, issued Mandates, binding constitutional rules, binding stop rules, binding closure states.
- **Class 2**: secondary contextual continuity narrative that may help handoff but does not create authority by itself.
- **Class 3**: proposals, ideas, hypotheses, suggestions, exploratory normalizations, and non-authorized chat content.

Class 3 material **must never** be treated as official architecture, continuity law, or implementation authority unless later elevated by explicit Director decision through the official documentary process.

### 2.3 Contradiction resolution

When contradiction appears:

1. executable evidence prevails over stale narrative for technical truth;
2. Director-approved authority prevails over unauthorized proposal;
3. Status / Closeout prevails over Plan for delivered state;
4. domain constitution prevails over lower narrative interpretation;
5. this CCD prevails over secondary continuity narrative for continuity law;
6. unresolved contradiction requiring invention of architecture or scope expansion must trigger **STOP** until Director instruction.

## 6. Official Block Protocol

The official documentary/implementation lifecycle of a block is:

1. Discovery  
2. Implementation Plan  
3. Independent Documentary Audit  
4. Documentary Commit  
5. Mandate  
6. Documented implementation or drafting execution within the Mandate scope  
7. Independent Audit of the execution result  
8. Status / Closeout  
9. Commit / publication only when separately instructed and authorized

### 6.1 Protocol rules

- No step may be skipped.  
- No closed step may be silently redone.  
- No block may be treated as officially delivered without its Status or Closeout authority.  
- No implementation or drafting may begin without its corresponding Mandate.  
- No Mandate may be inferred from discussion, Discovery, Plan, or this CCD alone.  
- No lower document may silently reopen a FULLY CLOSED block.  
- Audits remain READ ONLY.  
- Commits and pushes require explicit documentary authority and, where applicable, Director instruction.

### 6.2 Relation to documentary-only blocks

For documentary blocks such as the CCD:

- “implementation” means **documentary construction within a Mandate**;
- it does **not** imply Factory engineering implementation;
- technical systems remain untouched unless separately and explicitly authorized by another valid Mandate.

## 7. Git and GitHub Governance

GitHub is the sole shared official development reference.

### 7.1 Permanent GitHub rules

1. There is only **one official shared line** of development per official branch.  
2. No partial synchronization is official closure.  
3. Push is **not** a generic standing right created by this CCD.  
4. When a session is formally authorized for official closure, push becomes part of that closure checklist.  
5. Merge, rebase, reset, destructive remote operations, and similar actions remain prohibited unless explicitly authorized by the Director and/or the corresponding documentary process.  
6. Ahead/behind counts written in prose are informational only; Git must be re-verified READ ONLY before authoritative use.

### 7.2 Session-close reconciliation

Continuity restrictions against generic push remain valid. Therefore:

- this CCD does **not** authorize free push;
- an official block/session close requiring publication may require push **when** the Director or protocol has authorized remote closure of that block;
- until that publication step is completed and verified, the session is not officially closed.

### 7.3 Official Git baseline

The published HEAD of the official branch is the shared official continuity baseline.

If local and remote diverge at a moment when official continuity depends on shared closure, work must stop for Director instruction unless the open state itself is still an authorized in-progress state.

## 9. Block and Session Close

### 9.1 A session is not closed merely because work stops

A development or documentary session does **not** become officially closed because a person stops working.

### 9.2 Official close checklist

A session is officially closed only when all required items for that block are satisfied:

1. the block reached its intended state;
2. required documentation has been updated;
3. required audit has finished;
4. official Status or Closeout reflects the result;
5. official Commit has been performed if that stage is authorized;
6. official Push has been published if that stage is authorized for official shared closure;
7. published HEAD has been verified;
8. the working tree is clean for the relevant published corpus;
9. continuity documentation has been updated when required by the protocol.

### 9.3 New independent block rule

Until the official close checklist is satisfied for a block claiming closure, no **new independent block** may begin.

### 9.4 Status precedence

Status / Closeout remain the authority for what a block delivered and how it closed. This CCD defines the closure law, but does not replace those delivery records.

## 10. Canonical States

The following canonical states are recognized:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **COMPLETE** | Completed according to its defined scope |
| **PARTIAL** | Deliberately incomplete or limited relative to broader objective |
| **DEFERRED** | Postponed without being silently closed |
| **OPEN** | Still pending / unresolved |
| **PARKING** | Explicitly parked / not active |
| **NOT AUTHORIZED** | Outside authorized scope at the current stage |
| **FULLY CLOSED** | Officially closed with no pending deliverables inside that block’s scope |
| **NONE AUTHORIZED** | No active authorized next block or active implementation path |

### 10.1 State discipline

- FULLY CLOSED blocks must not be reopened by implication.  
- PARTIAL is not FULLY CLOSED.  
- PARKING is not implicit authorization.  
- NOT AUTHORIZED is binding.  
- NONE AUTHORIZED is binding until explicitly superseded by Director-authorized documentary process.

Future evolution is governed by **domains** and **programs** as documentary organization, not as new architecture.

This means:

- evolution is not interpreted as a disconnected list of isolated tasks only;
- documentary grouping may exist to improve coherence and traceability;

### 11.2 Mandatory limits

Any domain or program under this CCD:

- must derive from official blocks already recognized by Master Plan / Status / Closeout / Continuity record;
- must not compete with the Master Plan;
- must not create parallel constitutional or roadmap authority;
- must not silently turn session hypotheses into law;
- must not authorize anything by mere listing.

### 11.3 Purpose filter

Any future authorized evolution must be interpretable through the permanent purpose of chapter 1 and the stability/frontier rules of chapter 4.

### 11.4 No shadow Master Plan

This CCD governs **continuity law**, not the technical catalog of integration blocks. The Integration Master Plan remains the official roadmap authority for P-INT/Fase sequencing.

## 12. Anti-Redundant Audits

Repeated audits must be avoided when official documentary truth already exists.

### 12.1 Re-audit is not automatic

If a block already has a valid Status, Closeout, or Documentary Audit and no authorized change affects it:

- cite that authority;
- do not re-audit merely for convenience;
- do not treat re-discussion as new official evidence.

### 12.2 Re-audit is justified only when

- a document is being officially amended;
- a contradiction must be resolved;
- a new Mandate changes the relevant scope;
- the higher authority explicitly requires a new audit.

### 12.3 AI and human discipline

Both humans and AI assistants must prefer existing official Status/Closeout/Audit authorities over repeated rediscovery or unofficial reinterpretation.

## 13. AI Governance

This chapter binds future AI assistants regardless of model or vendor.

### 13.1 Mandatory reading order

Before acting on a continuity-sensitive task, an AI assistant must read in this order:

1. this CCD;
2. the relevant Status / Closeout of the active or referenced block;
3. the relevant continuity state record if active state matters;
4. the relevant Plan / Mandate;
5. Git verification if current branch/HEAD matters.

### 13.2 AI authority rules

An AI assistant must treat as authority only:

- executable truth for technical state;
- Director decisions and Mandates for authorization;
- domain constitutional corpus for Factory domain meaning;
- Status / Closeout for delivered state;
- this CCD for continuity law.

### 13.3 STOP conditions for AI

An AI assistant must stop and request instruction when:

- a contradiction cannot be resolved by the official hierarchy;
- a task would touch a prohibited system or frontier;
- a FULLY CLOSED block would need reopening;
- a push, merge, rebase, reset, or equivalent action is requested without valid authority;
- a second official authority would be created for the same category;
- a solution would require inventing architecture or expanding scope beyond official documents.

### 13.4 Authorization requests

An AI assistant must request authorization when:

- a Mandate is required and not issued;
- a closed block would be altered;
- a GitHub publication step is not already authorized;
- a documentary change would alter a FROZEN source;
- a task crosses from documentary plane into technical implementation.

### 13.5 Proposal discipline

An AI assistant may propose improvements only as non-authoritative proposals and must not:

- present them as approved architecture;
- treat them as law;
- apply them without authorization;
- convert conversations or ideas into official rules.

### 13.6 Reopening prohibition

AI assistants must not reopen FULLY CLOSED blocks or redefine their outcome unless an explicit higher-authority instruction exists.

## 1. Purpose

Constitute Strategic Program 03 — **Knowledge Alive** as the official Discovery instrument that defines **what** making Factory’s knowledge posture live means — and what it expressly does **not** mean — under Continuity §27 step 1 (Discovery).

## 2. Strategic objective / Vision

Make Factory’s **knowledge posture live**: Diamond / MPI / DDI-oriented knowledge completeness, **Evidence and Legitimacy as living requirements**, and Factory’s capacity to **hold and advance property intelligence** under constitutional Evidence/Legitimacy rules already published (Evolution Mandate Strategic Program 03).

**Vision (binding, result-only):** Factory evolves from a posture in which knowledge may still be carried by **synthetic / recorded / stub-scaffolded enrichment surfaces** already documented in Continuity and Integration (Continuity: architecture COMPLETE ≠ live enrichment; TD-HANDLERS stub/scaffold; RECORDED_ONLY packs) toward the Mandate SP03 result — **knowledge posture live** / **vitality of Factory knowledge**: Diamond / MPI / DDI-oriented knowledge completeness and **Evidence and Legitimacy as living requirements**, with Factory able to hold and advance property intelligence under those rules (Mandate SP03; Continuity Evidence and Legitimacy mandatory; FFO MOT-EVD / ST-EVD path; CB-06 / CB-07; FCC-CAP / MPI / DDI / Diamond lineage), **without** redesigning CB, motors, loops, swarms, AI Assist, Hardening, Integration, or P-INT catalogs.

Knowledge Alive is the Mandate phrase **vitality of Factory knowledge** (knowledge posture live). It is **not** commercial classification by Factory alone, **not** Marketplace publication, **not** Intelligence Alive completeness (SP04), and **not** Decision Engine Product sovereignty (SP05).

## 3. Why SP03 exists

1. **Mandate order** — After Factory Alive (SP01) and Arizona Alive (SP02), Evolution Mandate places **Knowledge Alive** as the next strategic destination: knowledge-rich Factory before Intelligence Alive, Decision Engine, Publication, Continuous Operation, and Scale Out.  
2. **Constitutional knowledge mission** — FCC-CAP / MPI / DDI / Diamond and Continuity (“Diamond as maximum priority”; “Evidence and Legitimacy mandatory”; FFO “Factory produce conocimiento — Producto monetiza”) already define **what** knowledge Factory must serve; SP03 constitutes the Evolution program that makes that **knowledge posture live** (Mandate) as a strategic result.  
3. **Published gap between construction and live enrichment** — Continuity records construction COMPLETE while **TD-HANDLERS** remains DEFERRED (stub/scaffold; do not confuse architecture COMPLETE with live enrichment) and Arizona ingest lineage remains **RECORDED_ONLY** / Offline where Live is Integration-separate. SP03 exists to govern Mandate **vitality of Factory knowledge** **without** treating those Integration/Hardening residuals as CB redesign.  
4. **Sequential non-collapse** — SP01 proved operable control plane; SP02 proved Arizona theater under reuse. Neither claimed Knowledge Alive complete (SP01-01; SP02-01). SP03 is the constitutional place for that claim — when later Continuity-gated evidence proves §4.
